DIABETES AND HBA1C

HbA1c is a blood test that provides an estimate of average blood glucose levels over approximately the previous 2 to 3 months.

HbA1c is commonly used by healthcare professionals to monitor blood glucose control and may be used as part of diabetes evaluation.

HbA1c results should be interpreted in the context of the individual's health history and other clinical information.

A healthcare professional should interpret an individual's specific HbA1c result and determine whether additional evaluation is appropriate.

Patients can ask their healthcare professional what their HbA1c result means in the context of their overall health.

Patients should not start, stop, or change medication based only on information provided by an AI assistant.
